# Code of Conduct

We want Repo Safari's issues, discussions and reviews to be a place where anyone can take part
without being harassed or talked down to.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what gets merged.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, including unsolicited promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, lock conversations, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, email the maintainers through the contact on the
[w20labs GitHub profile](https://github.com/w20labs), or use GitHub's "Report content" option.
Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
