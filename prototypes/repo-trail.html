    What this prototype tries

    • Each platform is a category. It shows the category's best-known repo, so walking the trail is a tour of what exists.
    • Rooms show the whole category. Press ↓ to drop into it. Every repo is a sticker, ranked by stars. Walk along the wall to compare stars, forks, issues, age and last commit, then open any of them on GitHub.
    • Saving is the goal. What you save becomes your trail, ready to share or come back to.
    • A little skill. This week's rising repos sit on a high platform at the end. You need a double jump to reach it.
    • Nothing to fail. No pit, no timer, no questions.
    ← → walk Space jump, twice in the air ↓ drop into the room E save Enter open on GitHub (in a room) Esc climb back out
